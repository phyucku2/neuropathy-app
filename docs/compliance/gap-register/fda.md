# FDA / Regulatory Gap Register (SaMD, Device Determination, Quality System)

> **This is a gap register prepared by engineering — NOT a regulatory determination, clearance, listing, or claim of FDA compliance.** The product is **NOT FDA-cleared, -listed, or -registered.** Whether the software (or its "improving/declining" trajectory output) is a §201(h) device / Software as a Medical Device is an **OPEN regulatory question that must be answered by a qualified FDA regulatory professional**, not by this document or by engineering. See [README](./README.md).

## Summary

This product is **NOT FDA-cleared, -listed, or -registered**, and whether its software (or its "improving/not-improving" trajectory output) is a §201(h) device / SaMD is an **open regulatory question** the repo correctly routes to a qualified FDA regulatory consultant (ADR-0003; reimbursement-signoff-packet §4/D2) rather than answering — this register preserves that and asserts no classification. What is demonstrably met-in-code is the defensive posture that keeps the non-device determination available: explicit non-diagnostic/decision-support disclaimers on patient and clinician surfaces, a deterministic explainable trajectory with AI narration guardrailed against diagnosis/dosage/causation and effectively off pending a BAA, and a recorded owner decision that BioMech hardware is billed/regulated separately. Everything downstream of a possible device finding — QMS/design controls, IEC 62304, ISO 14971 risk management, Part 11 e-records/signatures, premarket pathway, registration/listing, adverse-event/complaint handling, and clinical validation of the algorithm — is absent from the repo by design and is an organizational/regulatory action gated behind that determination, which the product has deferred to Wave 4 behind professional sign-off.

**Note on the AI narration status** (per adversarial review): narration is *effectively off* not because its feature toggle is off (the `ai_narrative` capability defaults to `True`, backend/app/services/capability.py) but because the BAA gate `ai_baa_confirmed` defaults `False` (backend/app/core/config.py) and no `ai_provider`/`ai_api_key` is set — the trajectory route ANDs these together (backend/app/api/routes/trajectory.py; ADR-0020). The off-request-path (post-response background task) and BAA-gate behaviours are verified.

## What this lens does NOT assert

- **It asserts no device classification.** Whether the software or its trajectory output is a §201(h) device / SaMD is an open regulatory question that must be answered by a qualified FDA regulatory consultant. Nothing here is a regulatory determination.
- The product is **NOT** FDA-cleared, -listed, or -registered; it is not clinically validated; it processes synthetic data only.
- "met-in-code" describes defensive engineering (disclaimers, deterministic explainable output, AI guardrails) that helps preserve the non-device option — it does **not** establish CDS carve-out eligibility, general-wellness status, or any regulatory conclusion.
- The QMS, design-control, IEC 62304, ISO 14971, Part 11, registration, and postmarket items are contingent on a device finding and are marked gap/organizational because they are absent by design pending that determination — their absence is not a defect for a confirmed non-device product.
