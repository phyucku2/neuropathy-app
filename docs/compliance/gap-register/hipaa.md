# HIPAA Gap Register (Security Rule, Privacy Rule, Breach Notification)

> **This is a gap register and remediation plan prepared by engineering — NOT a certification, attestation, or claim of HIPAA compliance.** All legal and regulatory determinations rest with qualified counsel and the covered entity. See [README](./README.md) for the honest product status and legend.

## Summary

This lens is a gap register, not a compliance attestation: the product is **NOT HIPAA-compliant or certified today.** It processes **synthetic data only**, has no deployed/configured production environment, no signed BAAs, and is not FDA-cleared. The codebase is unusually strong on the technical safeguards that can live in code — unique-user access control, consent-scoped authorization, an append-only PHI-free audit trail over reads and writes, Argon2id + JWT authentication with central revocation, ALCOA+ append-only integrity, a genuine right-of-access export (§164.524), and fail-closed gating of every PHI-to-third-party egress — but nearly all of these depend on organizational completion (BAAs, TLS, storage encryption at rest, risk analysis, workforce training, written policies) that has not been done. The most consequential remaining gaps are organizational/deployment prerequisites (P0: risk analysis, executed BAAs, a TLS-enabled and disk-encrypted production environment) plus a handful of code-adjacent gaps (accounting-of-disclosures and amendment workflows, inactivity timeout, cryptographic tamper-evidence, DB-level audit immutability).

Three Privacy Rule requirement families surfaced by adversarial review — §164.530 (privacy administrative requirements), §164.508 (authorization for uses and disclosures), and §164.522 (right to request restrictions) — have been added as explicit rows below.

## What this lens does NOT assert

- It does **not** assert the product is HIPAA-compliant, certified, or ready to handle real PHI. It processes synthetic data only.
- "met-in-code" means a control is demonstrably implemented at the cited path; it does **not** assert the control is operating in a production environment (none exists) or that its surrounding policy/procedure obligations are met.
- It makes no legal determination of covered-entity vs business-associate status, breach determinations, or the sufficiency of any notice — those rest with counsel and the covered entity.
- The FDA/SaMD row is informational only and asserts no device classification.
