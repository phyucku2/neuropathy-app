# SOC 2 Gap Register (Trust Services Criteria)

> **This is a gap register and remediation plan prepared by engineering — NOT a SOC 2 report, attestation, or claim of compliance.** A SOC 2 report can only be issued by a licensed CPA/AICPA-accredited firm after an examination over an audit period. See [README](./README.md) for the honest product status and legend.

## Summary

This lens is a gap register, not a SOC 2 attestation — the product is pre-production, processes **synthetic data only**, has no deployed environment, no signed vendor agreements, and no CPA engagement, so **no SOC 2 report is currently achievable.** The codebase is unusually strong on the technical criteria (CC6–CC8, C1, PI1): verified in-code RBAC with central revocation, Argon2id + kind-enforced JWTs, an append-only PHI-free audit log, a Fernet secret vault, blocking CI security/vuln/license gates, and validated ALCOA+ data integrity. The dominant gaps are organizational and environmental — CC1–CC5/CC9 policies, a risk-assessment and vendor/BAA program, operating monitoring/alerting, branch-protection evidence, and hosting-layer TLS + disk encryption — none of which code alone can satisfy; SOC 2 fundamentally requires an audit period and an independent CPA firm.

Adversarial review corrected one citation (the consent read predicate `_may_read_patient` lives in `backend/app/services/clinic.py`, not `capability.py`) and flagged that the optional **Privacy (P-series) category** was scoped out — captured as an explicit scoping row below.

## What this lens does NOT assert

- It does **not** assert the product is SOC 2 compliant or that any SOC 2 report exists or is achievable today; there is no CPA engagement and no audit period.
- "met-in-code" describes control design demonstrable at the cited path; SOC 2 Type II additionally requires evidence of operating effectiveness over time, which cannot exist without a running production environment.
- The Privacy (P-series) category is a scoping decision to be made with the auditor and counsel; its omission is deliberate, not a determination that Privacy is out of scope.
