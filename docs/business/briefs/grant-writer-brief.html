Neuropathy Care Platform — Grant Writer Brief

The raw material for a competitive SBIR/STTR application: the unmet need, the innovation, the specific aims a study could pursue, the fundable gap, and the preliminary work already done. Written so a grant writer can lift claims straight into a proposal — with each claim's evidentiary status marked.
As of: July 20, 2026 Targets: NIH SBIR/STTR — NINDS · NIDDK · NIA · NIBIB Applicant IP: Advanced Health & Wellness Group
Honesty rules for the proposal. Nothing here is validated yet: the product is not FDA-cleared, not clinically validated, and tested on synthetic data only. The Neuropathy Status Index is a non-diagnostic deterministic v1. That is not a weakness for a grant — the entire point of the SBIR ask is to fund the validation that does not yet exist. Frame the built system as strong preliminary/feasibility work and the study as what closes the gap. Do not assert reliability, responsiveness, MCID, or predictive accuracy the company has not measured; those are the aims, not results.

1 · The unmet need (significance)

  • Diabetic peripheral neuropathy (DPN) affects a large share of the diabetes population, drives falls, loss of function, and loss of independence, and is measured episodically and crudely in routine care — a monofilament test and a symptom question at an occasional visit.
  • Existing instruments (MNSI, NTSS-6, NPSI, Norfolk QOL-DN, Total Neuropathy Score, DN4, painDETECT, PROMIS) each capture one slice — symptoms or function or signs — and are administered as snapshots, not trajectories.
  • The gap the project targets: no validated, longitudinal, patient-owned measure fuses ADA-aligned symptom capture + objective gait/balance + labs into one explainable index the 60+ patient can maintain daily and hand to their clinician.

2 · The innovation

A non-diagnostic composite — the Neuropathy Status Index (NSI) — that fuses four streams no incumbent combines: patient-reported symptoms, objective balance/gait (device tier), phone/ watch mobility, and EMR labs (HbA1c), producing a daily trajectory with named, explainable signals rather than an episodic score. The scientific innovation the SBIR would fund is the development-and-validation pathway for this composite and the AI/ML layer (adaptive testing + multimodal fusion) that could improve its precision and predictive value.

3 · Candidate specific aims (for the writer to shape)

AimWhat it establishesMethodology anchor
Aim 1 — Content & construct validityThe NSI's domains map to established DPN constructs and patient-important conceptsFDA PRO guidance (2009) + PFDD series; concept elicitation, item generation, cognitive interviews; COSMIN
Aim 2 — Psychometric validationReliability, construct/criterion validity, responsiveness, and MCID of the compositeProspective cohort; IRT/Rasch or factor-analytic weighting vs a-priori; independent biostatistician
Aim 3 — Multimodal fusion & predictionWhether ML fusion of symptoms + sensor gait/balance + labs improves accuracy and predicts falls/progressionDigital-biomarker literature; GMLP + Predetermined Change Control Plan for any AI/ML SaMD

A phased frame reads cleanly for reviewers: deterministic v1 composite → proprietary multimodal data collection → ML-optimized/adaptive v2 → prospective validation → regulatory. Phase I proves feasibility (Aim 1 + pilot data); Phase II runs the powered validation.

4 · Preliminary work already in hand (the feasibility story)

  • A working, deployed system — FastAPI/Postgres backend, React web app, Android build; register, check-in, longitudinal record, the NSI, and an explainable trajectory all function end-to-end, now live on Azure staging (synthetic data).
  • A research-grade data model (ALCOA+) — append-only, superseding revisions, dual timestamps, full provenance (self-report / device / lab / wearable), LOINC/UCUM coding — i.e. the data-integrity backbone a validation study and an FDA file require, built before the study, not after.
  • EMR interoperability — patient-authorized SMART-on-FHIR import, registered with Epic's sandbox — evidence the lab-ingestion aim is executable, not hypothetical.
  • Measurement-science groundwork — an item bank, ADA construct mapping, and IRT/CAT scaffolding (ADR-0043) already laid, showing the team can execute the psychometric aims.
  • Engineering rigor unusual for the stage — binding CI quality gates, an adversarial internal security/function audit already run and its top findings fixed — de-risking the "can this team build and maintain regulated software" question reviewers ask.

5 · Team, environment & eligibility

  • Applicant / IP: Advanced Health & Wellness Group owns the IP; BioMech Health (Frank Fornari, PhD) is the licensee and gait/balance device partner — a credible source of the objective sensor stream and a domain-expert collaborator.
  • Registrations to complete before submission (all on the critical path, all inexpensive): SAM.gov / UEI, SBA Company Registry, eRA Commons, Grants.gov — plus SBIR small-business eligibility confirmation.
  • Consultants to name: an independent biostatistician (validation), an FDA regulatory consultant (device pathway), and a clinical PI (likely via a pilot site).

The claim discipline in one line: everything built is describable as feasibility; everything unmeasured (reliability, MCID, predictive accuracy, reimbursement value) is an aim or a citation, never a result. Reviewers reward a team that draws that line itself.
