1The problem & the approach

Peripheral neuropathy is common, progressive, and hard to track between visits. The signals that matter — how steady someone is on their feet, how they manage stairs and daily activities, and how their labs move — live in different places and rarely come together into a picture a patient or clinician can act on. Care decisions often rest on sporadic, subjective snapshots.

This platform brings those signals into one place and, critically, explains its output rather than issuing a black-box score. It fuses three independent streams — patient-reported daily function (ADL check-ins), labs pulled from the patient's own EMR via SMART on FHIR, and objective balance/gait measurement from BioMech reports — into a transparent trajectory (improving / stable / declining) with the specific signals and confidence behind it. It is a tracking and decision-support tool, deliberately non-diagnostic.

2Built and working today Live

Every item below is implemented, tested, and merged. To date the system runs on synthetic data (no real patient data has been processed) — the architecture, flows, and safeguards are real and exercised end-to-end.

Explainable trajectory engine

Deterministic direction + confidence + the named signals driving it — no black box, non-diagnostic by design.

Patient daily check-ins (ADL)

Fast walking / stairs / balance-confidence capture with same-day revision handling.

Lab intake (FHIR / LOINC)

Standards-based lab ingestion with reference ranges, ready for clinical vocabularies.

EMR patient pull (SMART on FHIR)

OAuth/PKCE patient-authorized import — the patient connects their own record.

BioMech report ingest

Balance/gait PDF reports parsed into research-grade, comparable observations.

Consent-gated clinician view

Panel, cross-source trend table, invitations — with a neutral privacy screen and patient-held consent.

AI narrative layer

Plain-language summaries, validated and off the request path, gated behind a business-associate boundary.

Server-enforced feature consent

Patients and clinics control data sources; the patient always holds the share-with-clinic switch.

Patient & clinician web apps

Accessible, mobile-width patient UI and a clinician surface, both verified in a real browser.

Production-ready operations

Containerized deploy, health/readiness, PHI-free logging & metrics, backup drill, incident-response runbook.

Engineering built to a regulated-software bar

Every change ships through an adversarial multi-reviewer process (security/privacy + correctness), with 100% backend test coverage including live-database integration tests, real-browser end-to-end verification of the UI, and a written decision record (ADR) for each architectural choice. Privacy is designed in: protected health information never enters logs or metrics, and access is consent-gated and audited. This is the kind of discipline diligence and compliance reviews look for.

4Why it can matter commercially

A large, underserved population

Peripheral neuropathy is highly prevalent — notably among people with diabetes — and existing tools rarely track functional decline between visits. (Founder to validate market sizing for the target segment.)

A reimbursement thesis, stated honestly

The device + self-report streams map naturally to Remote Therapeutic Monitoring. The clinic bills; the platform enables the capture and evidence. This is a pathway to validate, not a guarantee.

Objective + subjective, together

Pairing patient-reported function with objective balance/gait measurement is a differentiator over survey-only or wearable-only approaches.

Built for the compliance conversation

Privacy-by-design, consent-gated access, and documented operations shorten the path through security review and payer/provider diligence.

Honest status & what funding de-risks

The platform is pre-clinical and pre-revenue: it has run on synthetic data only, is not FDA-cleared, and its HIPAA-aligned architecture still needs formal compliance validation and executed business-associate agreements before handling real patient data. The reimbursement pathway is a researched thesis pending review by a certified professional coder and compliance/legal counsel, and a determination of the data source's regulatory (device) status. Funding is what moves these from "designed for" to "validated": clinical piloting, formal compliance certification, production EMR enrollment, and the reimbursement validation work.
