# Brainstorm #3 — Ingest + AI Health-Trajectory Analysis (all lenses)

**Date:** 2026-07-11
**Input:** requirements v1.1 + ADR-0003. **Protocol:** CLAUDE.md §6 — every canonical
lens + topic-specific lenses.
**Scope:** the re-centered product — ingest BioMech + Lab + ADL, graph, and AI-assess
health trajectory. Measurement is out (BioMech owns it).

---

## The shape of the thing

Three ingestion pipelines → one **unified longitudinal record** → **graphs** +
**AI trajectory analysis** → surfaced to patient (B2C) and/or clinician (clinical),
every feature toggleable.

```
 BioMech recordings ─┐
 Patient labs ───────┼─► normalize → time-align → unified record ─► graphs
 ADL / function ─────┘                                          └─► AI analysis
                                                                     (improving?)
```

The whole differentiator is the **AI analysis across modalities**. Ingest + graph is
commodity; the fusion + trajectory judgment is the product (and the patent surface —
ADR-0003).

---

## 1. Physician lens
- Clinicians already have BioMech's clinic app for movement data. Our value to them is
  the **cross-modal synthesis they don't get anywhere**: "gait score is up, but ADL
  self-report and HbA1c are worsening — net declining." That's a chart-review time-saver.
- AI output must be **explainable and sourced**: every "improving/declining" claim
  shows which inputs drove it and over what window. Clinicians will (rightly) distrust
  a black-box verdict.
- Keep the AI on the "surface the signal" side, not "make the call." Decision stays
  with the clinician (also the safer FDA posture).

## 2. Patient lens
- The promise patients want: *"Is what I'm doing working?"* A single, honest trajectory
  answer across their scattered data (movement reports, lab PDFs, how they're actually
  functioning) is genuinely novel *to them*.
- Bringing labs in must be dead simple: snap a photo of the lab printout / share a PDF;
  the app extracts values. Manual entry as fallback. Numb fingertips + low vision means
  camera + confirm beats typing.
- The trajectory result must never feel like a verdict of doom. "Declining" needs
  care: framed constructively, with what to discuss with the care team, never alarmist.

## 3. BioMech Health lens (client/licensee)
- We are explicitly **complementary, not competitive** now — we consume their data,
  we don't replicate their sensor. Good for the relationship and the license.
- V1 ingestion depends on their report format (documented). Request: synthetic sample
  PDFs now; API/SDK spec for V2. Our parser must tolerate their format changes
  (version the parser; fail safe on unknown fields).
- Opportunity: our AI trajectory layer could become a **selling feature for BioMech's
  own ecosystem** (their movement data made more valuable by fusion with labs/ADL) —
  which is exactly what a licensee wants. Worth positioning to them.
- > INVENTION CANDIDATE reminder: the fusion method is *ours* (ADR-0003), even though
  one input is their data. Keep the inventive analysis in this repo.

## 4. Apple developer lens
- No Core Haptics / measurement needed now — simpler. Core needs: document/photo import
  (PDFKit, VisionKit document scanner for labs & BioMech PDFs), on-device OCR (Vision
  framework) for extraction, HealthKit read for ADL-adjacent activity (steps, mobility,
  walking metrics that BioMech's app may also surface).
- On-device OCR/extraction is a privacy win (labs parsed locally, only structured
  values leave the device) and eases the AI/HIPAA story.
- PDF import + parsing is the V1 iOS workhorse.

## 5. Android developer lens
- Parity is easier now (no haptic fragmentation issue): CameraX + ML Kit document
  scanner & text recognition for lab/BioMech PDF capture; Health Connect for activity.
- Cross-platform is more viable for this ingest-centric app than it was for a
  measurement app — revisit the platform ADR with this lighter sensor footprint.

## 6. HIPAA / privacy lens
- **AI + PHI is the central risk now.** Any model that sees patient labs/ADL/BioMech
  data must be **BAA-covered**, or run **on-device**, or operate on **de-identified**
  data. No PHI to a non-BAA AI endpoint — hard line.
- Prefer: on-device extraction (OCR) → minimize what's sent → BAA-covered model for the
  analysis, with de-identification where feasible.
- Lab data is highly sensitive (may include genetic, reproductive, HIV, etc. markers) —
  extra care, granular consent, and the ability to exclude specific labs from AI.
- Audit every AI analysis run (inputs, model version, output) as a PHI-touching event.

## 7. SOC 2 / security lens
- Ingestion = new untrusted-input surface: uploaded PDFs/images are attacker-controlled.
  Sandbox parsing/OCR, validate/limit file types & sizes, treat extracted text as
  untrusted, malware-scan uploads.
- AI supply chain: pin model provider, log prompts/outputs (without leaking PHI to
  logs), guard against prompt injection from **document contents** (a malicious lab PDF
  could carry adversarial text — sanitize before it reaches the model).
- Secrets for the model provider in a managed secret store; per-tenant isolation of all
  ingested artifacts.

## 8. Marketing lens
- Positioning shifts cleanly: **"All your neuropathy data in one place — and an honest
  answer to 'is it working?'"** The AI trajectory is the headline; ingest is the enabler.
- B2C hook: bring your labs + your BioMech reports, see the whole picture.
- Clinical hook: cross-modal review + trajectory, RTM-friendly.
- Disclosure discipline: the *fusion/analysis method* is the invention — don't describe
  its mechanism publicly pre-filing (hard rule §3).

## 9. Legal & regulatory lens
- **FDA is the gating risk.** An AI stating health is "improving/declining" from
  clinical data trends toward **Clinical Decision Support / SaMD**. Whether it's exempt
  hinges on: is it for a clinician (who can independently review the basis) vs.
  directing patient action; does it "drive" clinical management; is the basis
  transparent. The **explainable, clinician-in-the-loop, non-directive** design (see
  Physician lens) is what may keep clinical use in the lower-risk CDS lane, and B2C in
  wellness. **Counsel/regulatory review required before launch** — own ADR.
- Lab data extraction accuracy has liability weight (a mis-OCR'd value feeding a
  trajectory). Require human confirmation of extracted lab values before they count.
- Copyrighted ADL instruments need licensing (as with earlier scales).
- AI vendor terms must permit health use and not train on our data; fold into the BAA.

## 10. Accessibility lens
- Lab import via camera must work for low-vision users: guided capture, large preview,
  read-back of extracted values ("HbA1c, 7.2 — correct?") via VoiceOver/TalkBack.
- Trajectory result needs a **non-color, non-graphical** expression (plain sentence +
  audio), not just an up/down chart — color-blind and low-vision primary persona.
- Keep AI language plain (6th–8th grade), literal, non-anxiety-inducing.

## 11. Payer / reimbursement lens
- Cross-modal review + documented clinician interaction still fits **RTM (98975–98981)**
  in the clinical version. The AI-generated, clinician-reviewed trajectory summary is
  strong documentation of the "review" the codes require.
- Ingested labs + ADL + BioMech in one reviewed record ≈ efficient monitoring evidence.

## 12. Data science / ML lens (now central)
- **Time-alignment is the core hard problem:** labs are sparse/irregular, BioMech
  recordings episodic, ADL periodic. Fusing them requires principled resampling,
  carry-forward rules, and explicit handling of missing/toggled-off sources. This
  alignment/weighting is a prime `> INVENTION CANDIDATE` locus (ADR-0003).
- **Hybrid design likely wins:** deterministic trend stats per signal (slopes,
  change-points, reference-range crossings) feeding an LLM that *explains and
  synthesizes* — rather than an LLM eyeballing raw numbers (which hallucinates trends).
  Numbers computed in code; narrative by model; every claim traceable to a computed stat.
- **Confidence & missingness must be first-class:** a trajectory from 1 lab + no ADL is
  not the same as from rich data — the output must say so. > INVENTION CANDIDATE:
  toggled-source-aware confidence model.
- Guard against prompt injection from document text (Security lens) at the ML boundary.
- No training on patient data without explicit consent + de-identification.

## 13. Clinical research / validation lens
- The trajectory model needs validation: does "declining" predict real outcomes? Design
  for retrospective evaluation from day one (store inputs, outputs, model versions).
- Explainability + versioned protocol/model = reproducible, publishable (post-filing).

## 14. Caregiver / family lens
- A plain-language trajectory ("Mom's overall trend this month: stable") is exactly what
  caregivers want — toggleable, consent-gated, no raw PHI in notifications.

## 15. Agile lens
- Re-sequenced for the ingest-centric product:
  - **Block A (foundation):** capability registry + toggles, auth, consent, audit,
    **unified longitudinal data model** (typed, timestamped, source-tagged, capability-
    state-tagged — the substrate everything needs).
  - **Block B (ingest + graph, B2C):** BioMech PDF parser; lab import (camera/PDF →
    on-device OCR → human-confirm → structured); ADL capture; per-source trend graphs.
  - **Block C (AI trajectory):** deterministic trend stats layer → BAA-covered LLM
    synthesis → explainable, sourced, confidence-scored trajectory; B2C wellness framing.
  - **Block D (clinical):** clinic tenancy, clinician review + toggles, clinician-in-
    the-loop trajectory, RTM documentation, data-to-clinic flow.
- Validation instrumentation baked into Block C, not bolted on later.

## 16. DevOps lens
- New infra: an **ingestion/parsing worker** (sandboxed, queue-driven, handles
  untrusted PDFs/images) and an **AI analysis service** (BAA provider, prompt/version
  logging, PHI-safe logs, rate/cost controls). Both isolated from the API.
- On-device OCR reduces server PHI exposure; server does fusion + model orchestration.
- Model/version pinning + full input/output capture for reproducibility &
  audit (serves SOC 2, HIPAA, FDA, and research at once — build once).

## Topic-specific lenses added this round (per §6): AI Safety/Trust; Data Interoperability

### AI Safety / Trust
- **Never present AI output as diagnosis.** Non-directive, explainable, confidence-
  scored, clinician-reviewable. Every trajectory shows its evidence and its gaps.
- **Hallucination control:** compute the numbers in code, let the model narrate only
  over provided stats; reject/flag any model claim not backed by a computed value.
- **Prompt-injection defense:** treat all ingested document text as untrusted; strip/
  sandbox before the model sees it.
- Recording this lens into future brainstorms' consideration per §6.

### Data Interoperability
- Model ingested data against **FHIR** resources (Observation for labs, etc.) even if
  we don't expose FHIR in V1 — it future-proofs clinic/EHR integration and V2 API.
- Lab imports vary wildly (labs, formats, units) — normalize units and map to LOINC
  where feasible for reliable trending.

## Decisions this brainstorm forces (ADR queue, updated)
1. **Unified longitudinal data model** (typed/timestamped/source-tagged/capability-
   aware; FHIR-informed) — foundation.
2. **Capability registry & toggle authority** (unchanged, still first-class).
3. **AI analysis architecture** — hybrid deterministic-stats + LLM synthesis; hosting
   (BAA vs on-device); explainability & confidence contract. *(Big one.)*
4. **FDA/regulatory posture of the AI trajectory** — CDS vs wellness; clinician-in-loop.
5. **Lab ingestion pipeline** — capture → OCR → human-confirm → normalize (units/LOINC).
6. **BioMech PDF parser** — format versioning, fail-safe.
7. **Platform strategy** — revisit; ingest-centric app makes cross-platform more viable.

- Time-alignment/weighting method for sparse, heterogeneous, irregular health signals.
